For your first hour with LSDB: when you came to do some science and would rather not spend that hour reading. Get something running below, then look things up as you need them.

Five minutes to a plot

No catalog downloads, no cluster. This works on a laptop over a conference wifi connection.

shell
pip install lsdb
⚡ triggers computation python 
import lsdb

gaia = lsdb.open_catalog(
    "https://data.lsdb.io/hats/gaia_dr3",
    columns=["ra", "dec", "phot_g_mean_mag"],
)

# One square degree is small enough to compute immediately.
cone = gaia.cone_search(ra=180.0, dec=10.0, radius_arcsec=3600)
cone.plot_points()

That is the whole shape of LSDB: open something enormous lazily, cut it down to something small, then compute.
